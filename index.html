What makes it useful

A temp mail app that feels like an actual product.

TempMail is focused on quick inbox creation, readable message detail screens, and fewer frustrating steps. It is built to help people move fast when they need a throwaway email address for testing, signups, or one-off verifications.

  • Disposable inboxes with private-domain support
  • Readable message screen with fit-first rendering
  • Mailbox renew and recovery flow for app-created inboxes
  • Legal, contact, and support paths kept easy to reach
